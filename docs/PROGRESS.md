## Decisions confirmed

- Store model: Single-Vendor.
- Primary database: PostgreSQL.
- Environments: local development and Docker Compose.
- API-first backend; frontend is a separate future client.
- Modular Monolith; not microservices.
- Authentication target: JWT access + refresh tokens in separate HttpOnly cookies with CSRF defenses.
- Initial target country/market: Iran.
- Store price and commerce amount unit: whole-integer toman amounts (`long` / PostgreSQL `BIGINT` / JSON integer).
- Identifier strategy: UUIDv7 generated by the application and stored as PostgreSQL `uuid`.
- Default store language/locale: Persian (`fa-IR`); store timezone: `Asia/Tehran`. Persist instants in UTC.
- Inventory reservation strategy: transactional `ReservedQuantity` counter maintained atomically with reservation records in PostgreSQL.

## Pending owner decisions

- Rounding policy for percentage/rate calculations that produce fractional toman results.
- Applicable Iranian tax rules and tax-inclusive/exclusive pricing policy.
- Guest checkout requirement.
- Frontend/API origin topology (same-site or cross-site).
- Initial payment, shipping, email/SMS and media storage providers.
- SKU uniqueness scope and whether fractional inventory is required.
- Stock reservation TTL is confirmed: default 15 minutes, configurable at store level. Expired unpaid reservations must be released idempotently. Backorder policy is configurable per product/variant, defaults to disabled (`NoBackorders`), and supports `NoBackorders`, `AllowBackorders` and `AllowBackordersNotify`.

The whole-toman amount representation is confirmed; the precise rounding rule for percentage/rate calculations must be finalized before pricing/tax implementation. SKU scope, fractional inventory, guest checkout, Iranian address details, provider choices and deployment origins remain deferred to the phases where they become necessary. No assumptions should silently become implemented business rules.
